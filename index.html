0.0s – 2.5s: The Kinetic Visual Pattern Interrupt

We deploy rapid high-contrast motion, unexpected physical scenarios, or high-urgency macro zooms that shatter the user's doom-scroll hypnosis before their brain can register it as an advertisement.

🎯

2.5s – 8.0s: Visceral Pain Amplification

We immediately present the acute, frustrating dilemma your ideal consumer experiences daily. The presenter or environment directly reflects the emotional state of the target demographic.

💡

8.0s – 18.0s: The Novel Mechanism Reveal

Consumers reject ordinary product pitches. We visually reveal the unique underlying mechanism (e.g. cellular absorption, patented weave, proprietary AI model) through exploded 3D cinematic animations.

26.0s – 30.0s: Frictionless Direct Call-To-Action
