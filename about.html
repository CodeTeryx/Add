Our Origin Story

The Agency That Stopped Burning Money on Soundstages

Back in 2021, our leadership ran a high-end commercial boutique in London and Bangalore. A simple 30-second cosmetic commercial required booking physical studios, shipping samples across customs, hiring 14 crew members, and praying that the weather held up. Total cost: $35,000. Total time: 6 weeks.

When diffusion networks matured in 2024, we pivoted completely. We took our knowledge of three-point lighting, color grading, sound design, and psychological pacing, and fused it with deep learning algorithms.

Today, Hyperframe operates as an agile, global creative powerhouse generating millions in revenue for our brand partners without ever compromising on artistic integrity.

The Studio Code:

1. Taste Over Tools

Anyone can type words into an AI box. Only masters of composition and pacing can craft an ad that sells $500,000 of inventory.

2. Commercial Cleanliness

We only utilize fully cleared training weights, commercially licensed soundscapes, and legally verified talent likenesses.

3. Measurable ROAS Accountability

If an ad looks gorgeous but fails to convert customers, it is a failed project. We measure success strictly through our clients' bottom-line revenue.
